## Description (≤ 4000)

<!-- field:description -->
HomeFit makes working out at home simple. Enter your height, weight, how many push-ups and pull-ups you can do and what equipment you have — the app builds a plan of basic exercises that matches your level.

HOW IT WORKS
• A short questionnaire: height, weight, push-ups, pull-ups and equipment — none, dumbbells, barbell or resistance bands.
• A built-in algorithm picks exercises, sets and reps. Everything is calculated right on your phone: no AI, no external services.
• We recommend 3 workouts a week, every other day, so your muscles have time to recover.

TECHNIQUE AT HAND
• Every exercise comes with photos of correct form.
• Step-by-step instructions: starting position, movement and what to watch out for.
• Exercise photos come from the open free-exercise-db (public domain).

WORKOUT LOG
• Mark the workouts you've completed.
• Look back at your history: what you did and when.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
• No sign-up, no account.
• No ads, no tracking.
• Your answers, plan and log are stored only on your device. Delete the app and the data goes with it.

PLEASE NOTE
HomeFit is not a medical app and does not replace advice from a doctor or a coach. If you have health issues or injuries, or haven't exercised in a while, check with a professional first. Stop the exercise if you feel pain, dizziness or discomfort.

Questions and feedback: deckkk491@gmail.com
