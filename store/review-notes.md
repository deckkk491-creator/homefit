## Notes (paste as is)

```
Hello, and thank you for reviewing HomeFit.

HomeFit is a home workout planner. No account or sign-in is required.

How to test:
1. Launch the app and fill in the short questionnaire: height, weight, number of push-ups and pull-ups, and available equipment (none / dumbbells / barbell / resistance bands).
2. The app builds a plan of basic exercises. Open any exercise to see technique photos and step-by-step instructions.
3. Start a workout and mark it as completed. It appears in the workout log.
4. Change the equipment in the questionnaire to see a different plan.

Data and privacy:
- The plan is generated by a simple rule-based algorithm that runs entirely on the device. The app does not use AI services and has no backend.
- All user data (questionnaire answers, plan, log) is stored only in local storage on the device. Nothing is collected or transmitted, so the App Privacy answer is "Data Not Collected".
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases.

Content:
- Exercise photos and instructions come from free-exercise-db (https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), released into the public domain (Unlicense).

Health:
- The app shows a health disclaimer: it is not medical advice and does not replace a doctor or coach. It does not diagnose, measure or treat anything and does not use HealthKit.

Contact: deckkk491@gmail.com
```
